Noto fonts the launcher gives games that ask for the console's system fonts (sceFontOpenFontSet),
under the names core/libs/prx/libSceFont/src/SystemFont.cpp looks for. SIL Open Font License 1.1: OFL.txt.

Source: https://github.com/notofonts/notofonts.github.io at d3abdda968b38bb6eb0ec59f6f9f58b8c2139bca,
fonts/<family>/unhinted/ttf/ (unhinted: FreeType scales them, and they are half the size of the hinted ones).

Japanese, Chinese and Korean (NotoSansCJK-*.ttc, about 76 MB) are not here: the launcher downloads them
from https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/releases/tag/Sans2.004 the first time a game needs them.
